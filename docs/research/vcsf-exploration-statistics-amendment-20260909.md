# Exploration Statistics Owner Amendment

## Authority And Scope

At 2026-09-09 12:23 UTC the owner accepted the recommendation to remove the
mandatory exploration bootstrap, stop its current computation, and simplify the
actual active goal. This is not cancellation of paired comparisons or the whole
ablation programme. The change applies prospectively to exploration decisions;
the earlier plans and partially computed statistics remain immutable history.

This supersedes the first-scale and operator-stage requirements that a complete
2,000-replicate paired-bootstrap panel must precede every stage decision. It also
supersedes earlier dated handoff statements that stop/amendment approval was
pending. It does not retroactively mark those old contracts passed.

## Current Decision Rule

- Retain all 5,000 images, the canonical sixteen targets, unrounded AP and all
  twelve evaluator summaries, source-excluded BB Mean, and matched comparisons.
- Use full-panel point-estimate changes, paired contrasts, complete factorial
  differences of differences, actual costs and implementation complexity for
  keep/change/reject decisions. AP is a dataset-level metric; do not invent
  per-image AP or average image-level AP as a substitute for official COCO AP.
- Require necessary prediction/input identity and metric verification. Reuse
  already qualified checks where identity is exact; do not recompute them only
  to satisfy a retired bootstrap prerequisite.
- Do not require bootstrap vectors, confidence intervals or significance tests
  for routine exploration. No significance or uncertainty claim follows from
  point-estimate differences. Final uncertainty work, if needed by a concrete
  retained claim, needs an explicit purpose and prospective scoped registration;
  it must not silently restart this retired statistical queue.
- Preserve all outcomes and the reused-validation/single-source limitations.
  No final promotion, added research matrix, result deletion or benchmark rerun
  is authorized by this amendment.

## Execution Partition

Stop only the currently identified serial first-scale CPU worker after checking
its PID/start identity, original workspace, output descriptors, plan/input hashes
and absence of child workers. Preserve its original root and all partial files.
Record an owner-directed stop separately, not a technical failure or successful
analysis completion. Do not modify the frozen producer or analysis runtime.

The existing first-pair and successor transition implementations still enforce
the old bootstrap contract. Their successor point-estimate-only path must use a
new plan identity and separate validation workspace, retain full-panel auditing
and exact historical reuse, and pass proportionate server ODA checks before
actual stage admission. Changing this policy does not make the old entry accept
partial input, nor authorize bypassing its checks or fabricating receipts.

The complete bounded programme, author-only final freeze, final-only five seeds,
affected six-source updates and release/documentation deliverables remain as
defined in [whole-ablation closure](vcsf-whole-ablation-closure-20260909.md).
The concise active-goal wording and its before/after API receipt are retained
under `outputs/diagnostics/codex_goal_revision_20260909/`.
